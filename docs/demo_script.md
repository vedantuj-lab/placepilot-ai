# PlacePilot AI — Hackathon Presentation & Live Demo Script
### Problem Statement: PS-01 (Scheme Saathi) | Team: Tech Dynamites

## Demo Flow (3-Minute Presentation)

### 1. The Core Philosophy (30 seconds)
- **Speaker:** "Namaste judges. Today we present PlacePilot AI — Scheme Saathi. Our core philosophy is simple: **RULES DECIDE. AI ASSISTS. HUMANS APPROVE.**"
- **Action:** Show the landing page. Point to the trust banner and the interactive Scheme Readiness preview.

### 2. The Hero Acceptance Criteria Workflow (90 seconds)
- **Speaker:** "Let's demonstrate the exact scenario: An undergraduate college student from Maharashtra whose family annual income is ₹1,50,000 searching for scholarships."
- **Action:**
  1. Click **AI Assistant** in the top navigation.
  2. Select the prompt:  
     `"I am a college student from Maharashtra. My family's annual income is approximately ₹1,50,000. Find scholarships that I may qualify for."`
  3. Observe the response: The Scheme Saathi Orchestrator executes 4 backend tools (`search_scholarships`, `check_eligibility`, `get_scheme_official_sources`, `get_required_documents`).
  4. Point to the **MahaDBT Post-Matric Scholarship for OBC Students** with 100% fee waiver.
  5. Click **View Detailed Eligibility Breakdown** to open the deterministic rule matrix. Point to the Gazette citations and condition comparison.

### 3. Document Readiness & Auto-Prefill (30 seconds)
- **Action:**
  1. Click **Document Readiness Studio**.
  2. Inspect the pre-loaded Maharashtra Domicile and Tehsildar Income Certificate. Show the AI OCR extracted certificate numbers.
  3. Click **Open Application Form Studio**.
  4. Show how the form is 100% prefilled automatically from profile data and extracted certificates without manual retyping.

### 4. Human Approval Center & Safe Portal Handoff (30 seconds)
- **Action:**
  1. Click **Review & Approve**.
  2. Explain: "Notice that the submission button is disabled until the citizen affirms the declarations. We never fabricate submission reference numbers."
  3. Check both affirmation checkboxes and click **Approve & Proceed to Official Portal**.
  4. The **Official Government Portal Redirection** modal appears, highlighting the verified `.gov.in` domain (`https://mahadbt.maharashtra.gov.in/`).
  5. Show the **Application Progress Tracker** where the status is now set to `Ready for Official Portal` with audit checksum `AUDIT-APPROVAL-...`.
